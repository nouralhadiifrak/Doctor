Services

Therapies & Rehabilitation

Each journey starts with a thorough assessment, followed by a therapy plan tailored to you or your child.

Speech Sounds

Correcting articulation difficulties such as lisps and sound substitutions, so speech becomes clear and easy to understand.

Fluency

Helping people speak smoothly and naturally, without excessive hesitations, blocks or repetitions, including stuttering.

Social Communication

Building social skills: understanding emotions, speaking up at the right moment and connecting with others more easily.

Language

Understanding and using words and sentences: language delays in children and aphasia in adults after a stroke.

Voice & Vocal Hygiene

For people whose voice tires or breaks easily: improving vocal technique and preventing future problems.

Literacy

Assessment and therapy for reading, writing and spelling difficulties, such as dyslexia and dysorthographia.
