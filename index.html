About me

A calm, caring space to communicate with confidence

I'm Fatine Tber, a speech and language therapist (orthophoniste) dedicated to helping people of all ages communicate with confidence. I work with young children, teenagers and adults facing speech, language, reading or fluency difficulties, including adults recovering their speech after a stroke or illness.

Every session is adapted to the person in front of me: play and stories for little ones, concrete everyday goals for teens and adults. I explain each step clearly, give simple exercises to continue at home, and involve families whenever it helps.
